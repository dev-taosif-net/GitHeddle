Action flow: preview, run, result and confirmations

Covers BULK-03..05 and SAFE-01..04 (ux-design §12). Every change goes preview → run → result. The dialog closes into a job; progress and results live in the docked job panel (Mod+J) above the bottom bar (R10). Plain Enter never submits; Mod+Enter runs non-destructive actions only (R20).

1. Preview: Sync with dev

app-front has uncommitted files, so its row is tinted until Stash or Skip is chosen; nothing is preselected (R17). app-api is mid-merge, so it sits under "Skipped (1)" with a fix link. The footer sentence explains why the primary is not ready (R18).

4. Protected branch and force with lease (SAFE-01, SAFE-02)

One block per preview, pinned above the footer, at most one checkbox per kind of risk. No typing to confirm (R21). Plain --force is never offered.

5. Destructive confirmations (SAFE-03)

A loss list on danger-soft, the button named after the loss, [Stash instead] where uncommitted changes would be lost, focus on Cancel. Neither Enter nor Mod+Enter confirms.

6. Preview loading, errors and nothing to do

Rows render at once with "Checking…" and fill in as plans arrive (PERF-03). A failed check is unticked with a three-part message; a stale preview shows a strip but the core's preflight is the real guard (SAFE-07).

7. Paused result and quitting while writing

Conflicts are a pause, not a failure; Retry failed leaves them out (R19).
