Conflicts screen and merge editor

Covers CONF-01..11 (ux-design §10 and §11). Merge sides are teal (Incoming) and violet (Current), never green and red (R12). The example is app-api merging TK-2636-taosif-Onboarding into dev, paused by "Sync with dev", with 3 conflicted files; Api.csproj has 6 conflicts.

1. Conflicts screen

One card per repo in a merge, rebase or cherry-pick. The ▾ menu on Api.csproj is open: "Use … for all" resolves every conflict with that side; it is not a whole-file checkout (R36). Continue merge is aria-disabled with its reason in visible text. Abort is a danger-text button, always visible.

10. Rebase variant

During a rebase the sides swap meaning (§11.2): Incoming is your own commit being replayed, Current is dev plus your commits already replayed. The file bar says which commit is being replayed, Incoming carries the "Your commit" tag, and the coach strip has its own rebase copy (shown once for rebases, §11.8).
